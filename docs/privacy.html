This policy explains what information the ግቢ ጉባኤ app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it as small as possible: the app is a spiritual-content app for our fellowship, not an advertising product.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account). Your e-mail address, your name, and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account name and e-mail. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.
  • Profile details you choose to give. Whether you are a student or a graduate, and for students your department and year of study.
  • Favorites. The hymns, sermons, synaxarium days, photo albums and events you mark as favorites, so they follow you to a new phone.
  • Notification information. To send announcements, the app subscribes your phone to Firebase Cloud Messaging "topics" (such as your language). A device identifier is created by Firebase for this purpose.
  • Settings on your phone. Your language, theme, text size and notification choices are stored on your device only.

You can use most of the app (listening, reading, hymns, photos, events) without an account. We do not collect your location, contacts, or photos from your phone. Administrators of the fellowship who upload pictures or audio choose those files themselves.

2. How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your favorites and profile.
  • To send you announcements and event reminders you have not turned off.
  • To understand who the app serves (for example, how many students and graduates) so that fellowship leaders can plan activities.

We do not sell your information, show advertising, or share it with third parties for their own use.

3. Services we rely on

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging) to store data and deliver notifications. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Who can see your information

Your favorites are visible only to you. Your profile (name, status, department, year, e-mail) can be seen only by you and by the Apps's administrators. Public content (sermons, hymns, announcements, events, photos) is visible to everyone who uses the app.

5. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your account information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Menu → Settings → Account → "Delete my account". This permanently removes your account, your profile and your favorites. If you cannot use the app, e-mail us at the address above and we will delete it for you.

Notification subscriptions end when you turn notifications off in Settings or uninstall the app.

6. Children

The app is intended for university students and graduates and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase security rules that allow each person to read and change only their own account data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
